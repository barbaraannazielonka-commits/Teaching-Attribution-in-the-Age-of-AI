Why this matters more now

Attribution does three jobs. It gives credit to people whose work you used. It lets readers check your claims. And it shows which thinking is yours, which is how a teacher can tell what you’ve learned.

AI adds a fourth problem: chatbots can state false things with total confidence, including made-up sources. In 2023, lawyers in the U.S. federal case Mata v. Avianca filed a brief citing court cases that a chatbot had invented, and the judge sanctioned them. The lesson isn’t “never use AI.” It’s that you are responsible for every claim you hand in, no matter where it came from.
